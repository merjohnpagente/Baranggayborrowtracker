## Icons

Everything in `assets/icons/` is original vector artwork authored for this
project. It contains no third-party material.

> These are stock photographs used as **placeholders** so the catalogue is
> usable before the barangay supplies its own pictures. They do not depict the
> real barangay, its vehicles or its facilities, and should be replaced with
> approved local photographs before any public release.
